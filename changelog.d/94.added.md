Added the MHCCL model for cluster-level contrastive learning, ported from [MHCCL](https://github.com/mqwfrog/MHCCL). A query encoder and a momentum-updated key encoder embed two augmented views; FINCH hierarchical clustering partitions those embeddings from fine to coarse with no cluster count to choose; and each anchor is contrasted against both the instances sharing its finest-grained cluster and every partition's prototypes, under multi-label sigmoid objectives rather than softmax InfoNCE. Upward masking drops per-cluster outliers before a prototype is recomputed, and downward masking moves candidate negatives sharing the anchor's parent cluster into the positives. It accepts an injected `AugmentationProducer[ViewPair]` like the other contrastive models and supports both `VECTOR` and `SEQUENCE` output. FINCH is reimplemented in torch on the input's device, the reference's scikit-learn, SciPy and pynndescent path not being a dependency here; connected-component labelling is asserted equal to `scipy.sparse.csgraph`. Clustering runs per batch over each sample's raw series and both views unioned with a momentum feature bank, because the reference addresses its per-epoch assignments through a dataset-global sample index this library's batch contract does not carry; that bank is capped at one epoch of rows, making `feature_bank_size` a ceiling the data can lower. Candidate scores are inner products computed with `einsum`, where the reference reshapes its `(B, M, D)` candidate stack to `(B, D, M)` and so reinterprets the flat buffer rather than permuting axes. Ten further divergences, a singleton-batch guard via `ensure_pairable_batch`, and one open limit are recorded in the *Current divergences and known limits* section of the contributing guide.
