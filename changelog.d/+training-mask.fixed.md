**Behaviour change:** TS2Vec and AutoTCL now apply their configured `mask_mode` (default `BINOMIAL`) while training, as upstream does. The dilated encoder `forward` defaulted to `mask_mode=ALL_TRUE`, which overrode the configured mode, so since the first port these models trained without any input mask. The encoder `forward` now defaults to `None`: the configured mode in training, `ALL_TRUE` in eval and `encode()`. The AutoTCL augmentation step still encodes without a mask, as upstream does. CoST is unchanged: upstream CoST applies no mask in training, and its `mask_mode` argument has no effect there either. Training results for TS2Vec and AutoTCL change.
