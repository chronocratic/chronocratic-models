Added a `num_covariate_channels` argument (default `0`) to `TimeNet`, `RecurrentAutoEncoder`, `TimeVAE`, `TST` and `Series2Vec`, and to their config dataclasses, for inputs laid out as `[covariates | raw channels]` as the forecasting datamodules produce. The leading channels are still fed to the encoder but are excluded from the reconstruction loss, since covariates are known inputs rather than targets. For `TimeVAE` both the elementwise and the per-axis reconstruction terms skip them, and for `TST` they are never masked, so they are never scored either, with the mask still drawn over every channel so the random stream is unchanged. For `Series2Vec` the soft-DTW and frequency-distance targets are computed on the raw channels only, while the encoder still sees all channels. The decoder keeps its full width, so layer shapes, state dicts and checkpoints do not change, and the default `0` leaves every loss identical to before. Values outside `0 <= num_covariate_channels < input_dim` raise `ValueError`. For `TimeVAE` the reconstruction term is a sum over channels while the KL term is not, so with `num_covariate_channels > 0` the KL term gains relative weight by about `C/(C-k)` times (8 times for a univariate series with 7 covariates). This matches upstream `TimeVAE` on a truly univariate series, so `reconstruction_weight` is left unchanged, but `TimeVAE` results with `k > 0` are not like-for-like with earlier runs; scaling `reconstruction_weight` by about `C/(C-k)` approximates the old balance, though not exactly because the per-axis term does not scale with the channel count.
