Added a `num_covariate_channels` argument (default `0`) to `TimeNet`, `RecurrentAutoEncoder`, `TimeVAE`, and `TST`, and to their config dataclasses, for inputs laid out as `[covariates | raw channels]` as the forecasting datamodules produce. The leading channels are still fed to the encoder but are excluded from the reconstruction loss, since covariates are known inputs rather than targets. For `TimeVAE` both the elementwise and the per-axis reconstruction terms skip them, and for `TST` they are never masked, so they are never scored either, with the mask still drawn over every channel so the random stream is unchanged. The decoder keeps its full width, so layer shapes, state dicts and checkpoints do not change, and the default `0` leaves every loss identical to before. Values outside `0 <= num_covariate_channels < input_dim` raise `ValueError`.
