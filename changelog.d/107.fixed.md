**Behaviour change:** SimCLR's default augmentation now gives better features than an untrained encoder. Its reference strong view permutes segments, and because SimCLR's loss compares the two views timestep by timestep, the encoder learned to ignore where things happen in a series (on InsectWingbeatSound, 40% against 58% for an untrained encoder). `SimCLR` now z-normalizes each series over time before augmentation and in `encode()` (`instance_normalize=True`, also on `SimCLRModelParameters`). The default pair is now per-timestep `TimestepScaling` (weak) and `Jitter(0.8)` (strong) with segment permutation off (`_default_simclr_pair(max_segments=..., jitter_sigma=...)` sets both); the previous per-series `Scaling` was cancelled by the encoder's GroupNorm. The reference's strong view is `augmentation=_default_simclr_pair(max_segments=5)`. Checkpoints trained with the old defaults encode differently, because `encode()` now normalizes its input; pass `instance_normalize=False` to load them unchanged.
