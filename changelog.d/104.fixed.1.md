**Behaviour change:** training is now repeatable under a fixed seed. Every random draw in the library now comes from the global torch generator, so `lightning.seed_everything` (or `torch.manual_seed`) controls all of it; no NumPy or Python `random` generator is used anywhere. Previously three NumPy generators were never seeded: the training crop of `process_sample_length` (TS2Vec, AutoTCL, CoST, TST, MHCCL, SimCLR, TimeVAE when `max_train_length` is set), the binomial and continuous masks of the dilated encoder (which never ran in training before the masking fix below), and the default TS2Vec crop-and-shift views. The CoST temporal index draw moved from a NumPy generator to torch as well. Draws use the CPU generator, so they are identical on CPU, CUDA and MPS. Results for these models change once (new random streams) and are reproducible from then on; earlier numbers for them cannot be reproduced exactly.
